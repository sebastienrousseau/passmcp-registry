## Highlights ⭐️

* **In lockstep with passmcp 0.0.10 and passmcp-reporting 0.0.10**: the family's tenth release. The scorecard now runs passmcp 0.0.10 (`catalog.toxic_combination` closed-world fixture exclusions, `catalog.tools.annotation_honesty` read-only `run_*` and `execute_*` inspection allowances, `catalog.tools.idempotency` plural wording, and `telemetry.Redactor.KeepIdentifier` identifier preservation), with `MethodVersion` staying 1 and DISCLOSURE.md rules unchanged.
* **15-repository family table in the README**: adds the six Apache-2.0 MCP language SDKs (`passmcp-sdk-go`, `passmcp-sdk-python`, `passmcp-sdk-java`, `passmcp-sdk-cpp`, `passmcp-sdk-typescript`, and `passmcp-sdk-rust`) alongside the existing nine family repositories at `0.0.10`.
